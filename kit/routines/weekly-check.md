# Weekly check (about 15 minutes)

Once a week, copy the text below into a new chat in your Project. Then paste the rows of your **Deadlines**, **Opportunities** and **Contacts** sheets under it. You can select the rows in the sheet and copy them as they are.

---

Weekly check. Today is [date]. Below are my Deadlines, Opportunities and Contacts rows.

Tell me, in this order:

1. **Due in the next 14 days** (Deadlines with status Open): what, when and whether the date is checked. Put anything due in 3 days or less first.
2. **Overdue**: Open rows whose date has passed. Ask me whether each is done, moved or not needed.
3. **Decisions I need to make**: Opportunities with status Idea or Read whose deadline is within 30 days.
4. **Not checked**: deadlines within 30 days that are still Not checked, with the link to check.
5. **Waiting**: Contacts where Waiting on is Me (I owe a reply or a next step), then Them where the last contact was more than 14 days ago.

Then give me any row updates as rows I can paste over the old ones. Do not invent anything. If a row is unclear, ask about it at the end.
