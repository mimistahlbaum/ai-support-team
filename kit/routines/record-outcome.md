# Record what happened

Use this after you apply for something, after you hear back or once a year to see the whole picture.

## After you apply or hear back

Copy the text below into a chat in your Project.

---

Update my records. What happened: [for example "applied to the Nordic residency today" or "unsuccessful at the city arts grant, email below"]

Give me:

1. The updated **Opportunities** row (Status: Applied, Waiting, Successful, Unsuccessful or Not applying, with the date).
2. Any **Deadlines** rows to mark Done, and any new ones (for example a report or acquittal, the report a funder asks for after a grant).
3. If there is feedback in the email, a two-line summary in Notes.

[paste the email or details here]

---

## Once a year

Paste all your Opportunities rows and ask:

---

Summarise my year of open calls from the rows below: how many I looked at, applied for and got, by type (grant, residency, audition, other) and by status. Count only what the rows say. Then list in three lines what seems to work and what does not, based only on these rows.
