# Check an open call

When you find a grant, residency, audition or open call, copy the text below into a chat in your Project with the link. This works best when your assistant can open web pages. If it cannot, copy the text of the organiser's official page yourself and paste it in.

---

Check this open call for me: [link or pasted text]

1. Find the organiser's own page for this call. If the link I gave is a newsletter, listing or social post, say so and find the official page if you can.
2. From the official page only, tell me:
   - the deadline, with time and time zone, converted to my time zone
   - who can apply, quoting the eligibility words. Say clearly whether I can apply from where I live.
   - what they give (money, time, space) and what they ask for
   - what I must submit
3. Mark each point **Checked** (read on the official page, with the link) or **Not checked**.
4. Give me one row to paste into **Opportunities**, with Status = Read.

Do not guess anything the page does not say. If the page could not be opened, say so and mark everything Not checked. If I pasted the text, count it as the official page only if I say I copied it from there.

If I then decide to apply, I will tell you, and you give me the updated row (Status = Will apply) and a **Deadlines** row for the application.
